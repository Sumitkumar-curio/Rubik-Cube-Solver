How to turn the cube (FRONT = home)
  1. FRONT — face camera · UP at top of photo
  2. RIGHT — from FRONT, turn anti-clockwise (from above) · UP stays on top
  3. BACK — turn anti-clockwise once more (FRONT→right→back) · UP on top
  4. LEFT — from FRONT, turn clockwise · UP on top
  5. UP — back to FRONT, tip cube away · FRONT edge at bottom of photo
  6. DOWN — back to FRONT, tip cube toward you · FRONT edge at top of photo
